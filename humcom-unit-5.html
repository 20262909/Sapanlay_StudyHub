Advanced Design Issues and Captology

Unit 5 Overview — What to Expect

Support users with Proactive, Reactive, and Timely UX; build truthful visualizations following Shneiderman's Mantra; and apply B.J. Fogg's Captology ethically.

Learning Objectives

  • Compare Proactive Support, Reactive Support, and Timely UX along the Help Spectrum.
  • Apply Shneiderman's Mantra ('Overview first, zoom and filter, then details on demand') and avoid truncated y-axes.
  • Distinguish Macrosuasion from Microsuasion and prevent the Boomerang Effect in persuasive systems.

Lesson 5.1: Advanced Design Issues: Timely UX & Data Visualization

Jump to Quiz 5.1 ↓
Key Reminder: Deceptive Chart Warning: Never truncate a bar chart's y-axis (e.g., starting at 95% instead of 0%) or use 3D tilt that distorts numeric comparisons.

Proactive, Reactive, and Timely UX

  • Proactive Support: Anticipates hesitation before the user asks (hover tooltips).
  • Reactive Support: Searchable FAQs/Help Centers sought out when stuck.
  • Timely UX: Contextual inline validation delivered inside the form at the exact moment an error occurs.

Shneiderman's Visual-Information-Seeking Mantra: "Overview first, zoom and filter, then details on demand."

Lesson 5.2: Introduction to Captology (Persuasive Technology)

Jump to Quiz 5.2 ↓
Key Reminder: Boomerang Effect Warning: Telling low-energy households they use less power than neighbors can cause them to relax and increase usage unless paired with positive social praise.

Macrosuasion, Microsuasion & Fogg's Functional Triad

Coined by B.J. Fogg, Captology studies computing products designed to change attitudes or behaviors without coercion. Macrosuasion describes whole-product persuasion (e.g., a meditation app), while Microsuasion embeds small nudges inside neutral tools (profile completion bars, study streaks, praise banners).

Persuasive Tool Strategies: Reduction (one-click actions), Tunneling (step-by-step wizards), Tailoring (personalized content), Suggestion (timely prompts), and Self-Monitoring (progress bars & streak counters).
