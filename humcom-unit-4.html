Unit 4 Overview — What to Expect

Design across Don Norman's Visceral, Behavioral, and Reflective emotional levels; handle errors empathetically; and master the UX tool pipeline and seven ethical responsibilities.

Learning Objectives

  • Analyze products across Don Norman's Visceral, Behavioral, and Reflective emotional levels.
  • Design empathetic error messages and avoid the Uncanny Valley in anthropomorphic avatars.
  • Apply IA tools (Card Sorting, Tree Testing), Hotjar analytics, and Privacy by Design.

Lesson 4.1: Emotional Interaction & Design

Jump to Quiz 4.1 ↓
Key Reminder: Uncanny Valley Warning: Overly lifelike human avatars that fall short of natural human behavior trigger discomfort and distrust.

Don Norman's Three Levels of Emotional Design

1. Visceral Level

Immediate, instinctive reaction to visual beauty, typography, and sound before interaction.

2. Behavioral Level

Pleasure, responsiveness, and sense of control experienced while actively using the product.

3. Reflective Level

Conscious pride, self-image, and long-term personal meaning attached to using the product.

Replace cold machine errors (FATAL ERROR 0x8004) with warm, plain-language guidance that reassures users and provides a one-click fix.

Lesson 4.2: Interaction Design Tools & Ethical Implications

Jump to Quiz 4.2 ↓
Key Reminder: Surveillance Warning: Always mask sensitive user inputs (passwords, credit cards, health records) in session-recording tools like Hotjar.

Information Architecture, Hick's Law & UX Pipeline

Interaction Cost is the sum of physical clicks/scrolls and mental effort. Under Hick's Law, decision time grows logarithmically with the number of choices. Key tools include OptimalSort (Card Sorting), Treejack (Tree Testing), Figma (wireframing, multiplayer prototyping, Dev Mode), Hotjar (heatmaps & session recordings), and axe/Lighthouse (accessibility audits).

Ethical Responsibilities: Uphold Privacy by Design, audit for algorithmic bias, leverage the Curb-Cut Effect (accessibility features benefiting everyone), and practice Sustainable UX.
