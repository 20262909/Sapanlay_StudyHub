Using and Supporting Printers

Unit 3 Overview — What to Expect

Diagnose and resolve common laser and inkjet printer hardware and spooler issues, including paper jams (13.00.00), fuser errors (50.x), laser scanner errors (52.00), and network spooler errors (0x0000007C).

Learning Objectives

  • Follow safe power-down and cooling procedures before servicing printer fuser and laser assemblies.
  • Diagnose common printer error codes and apply targeted hardware or Print Spooler fixes.

Lesson 3.1: Printer Maintenance & Common Error Codes

Jump to Quiz 3.1 ↓
Key Reminder: High-Temperature Fuser Warning: Laser printer fuser assemblies operate at extreme heat—always power off and let the unit cool for 30 minutes before servicing a 50.x Fuser Error.

Common Printer Error Codes, Causes & Fixes

Error CodeMeaningTroubleshooting Procedure
13.00.00 / JammedPaper JamPower off, gently pull stuck paper along the feed path without tearing, and restart.
50.x (50.4, 50.7)Fuser ErrorTurn off, let cool for 30 minutes, plug printer directly into wall outlet (not UPS), or replace fuser.
79 ErrorFirmware / Spooler ErrorClear print queue, disconnect network/USB cables, and power cycle the printer.
52 / 52.00 ErrorScanner / Laser ErrorUnplug for 2 minutes and restart; replace laser scanner assembly if persistent.
0x0000007CNetwork Connection ErrorRestart the Windows Print Spooler service (services.msc) or re-add printer via IP address.
Toner / Ink MissingCartridge Read ErrorClean copper contacts with a dry lint-free cloth and re-seat cartridge firmly.
Offline StatusCommunication LossUncheck "Use Printer Offline," verify USB/Wi-Fi link, and restart Print Spooler.
59.F0 ErrorMain Motor / Belt LockRe-seat toner and transfer unit; perform a 5-minute cold reset.
