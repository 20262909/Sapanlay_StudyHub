Lesson 4.1: CompTIA A+ 7-Step Troubleshooting Framework

Jump to Quiz 4.1 ↓
Key Reminder: Single-Variable Rule: Never change multiple hardware parts simultaneously during testing—you will not know which change fixed or broke the system.

The 7 Steps of the CompTIA A+ Troubleshooting Methodology

  1. Identify the Problem: Gather user symptoms, check error codes, and verify recent changes.
  2. Establish a Theory of Probable Cause: List likely causes starting from the simplest/most obvious.
  3. Test the Theory to Determine the Cause: Run quick diagnostic checks to confirm or eliminate your theory.
  4. Establish a Plan of Action & Identify Potential Effects: Plan the safe repair procedure and back up critical data first.
  5. Implement the Solution or Escalate: Apply the repair or escalate to a senior technician if needed.
  6. Verify Full System Functionality & Implement Preventative Measures: Confirm full boot/operation and prevent recurrence.
  7. Document Findings, Actions, and Outcomes: Log root cause and resolution in the service ticket for future reference.

Common Beginner Mistakes to Avoid

Avoid skipping data backups, changing multiple variables at once, ignoring simple cable checks, replacing hardware without testing, skipping ESD grounding, working on live powered systems, forcing RAM/cables backwards, over-applying thermal paste, and skipping documentation.

Lesson 4.2: Motherboard POST Beep Codes & Diagnostics

Jump to Quiz 4.2 ↓
Key Reminder: RAM Seating Tip: When troubleshooting 1–3 short POST beeps, power off, unplug, clean gold RAM contacts with isopropyl alcohol, and firmly re-seat modules until latches click.

Common Computer POST Error Beep Codes

Beep PatternFaulty ComponentTroubleshooting Action
1, 2, or 3 Short BeepsDRAM / RAM MemoryRe-seat RAM sticks, clean gold contacts with isopropyl alcohol, test one stick at a time.
4 Short BeepsMotherboard Timer / CMOSClear CMOS by removing the coin-cell battery for 5 minutes.
5 Short BeepsCPU / ProcessorCheck 4/8-pin CPU power cable, re-seat CPU, and apply fresh thermal paste.
6 Short BeepsMotherboard / Keyboard ControllerTest a different keyboard; inspect motherboard PS/2 or USB controller.
7 Short BeepsCPU / MotherboardUpdate BIOS via flashback tool or inspect CPU socket pins.
8 Short BeepsGraphics Card (GPU)Re-seat GPU in PCIe slot, verify 6/8-pin PCIe power cables, or test integrated graphics.
